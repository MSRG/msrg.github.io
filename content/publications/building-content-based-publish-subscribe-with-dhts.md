P2P-ToPSS extends the Toronto Publish/Subscribe System (ToPSS) to peer-to-peer
environments by building content-based routing on top of distributed hash tables.
The paper presents the routing algorithms, subscription model, and the workload
package used to evaluate the system at scale.
