Fledge addresses a common gap in federated-learning evaluation: most benchmarks
either simulate the system or assume data-center style deployments. This work
instead studies federated learning in more realistic edge settings, including
hardware diversity, energy efficiency, differential privacy, and heavy client
dropout.
