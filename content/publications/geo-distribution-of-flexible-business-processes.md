Flexible business processes increasingly span participants and data across
organizations and geographies. This paper presents a fully geo-distributed workflow
engine that implements the Guard-Stage-Milestone meta-model on top of PADRES, with
a context-aware mapping (CAM) that improves throughput and latency by up to 5x
compared to a baseline mapping. The online appendix and companion material are
archived as a separate data set entry.
