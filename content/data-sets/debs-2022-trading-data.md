This benchmark contains 289 million tick events from three European exchanges,
covering November 8–14, 2021. The daily files total approximately 25 GB.

The [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/6382482) provides the files,
checksums, and licensing terms. Cite the accompanying DEBS 2022 paper when
using the dataset.
